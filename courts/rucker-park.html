Rucker Park

A Harlem streetball landmark named for Holcombe Rucker, the playground director who began a citywide youth basketball tournament there in 1950. It is one of the best-known outdoor courts in the United States and is listed with lights for evening play.

Quick answer

Rucker Park is a free public outdoor basketball court at 280 West 155th Street in Harlem, New York City. It is named for Holcombe Rucker, the playground director who started a citywide youth tournament there in 1950, and it is one of the most widely recognized outdoor courts in the United States.

Questions

Straight answers, each supported by the sources above.

Where is Rucker Park?
At 280 West 155th Street in Harlem, New York, NY 10039. It is a New York City public park.
Is Rucker Park free?
Yes. Rucker Park is a public New York City park and access to the outdoor courts is free.
Who was Holcombe Rucker?
Holcombe Rucker was a New York City playground director who founded a citywide youth basketball tournament in Harlem in 1950. The park was renamed in his honor.
Can you play at Rucker Park at night?
The park is documented as having court lighting, so evening play is possible. NYC Parks does not publish posted court hours for the site.
